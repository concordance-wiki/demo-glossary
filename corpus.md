# Corpus

The notes and documents of every [source](source.md) a [build](build.md) reads together: what the [dictionary](dictionary.md), the [search index](search-index.md) and the [alphabetical index](alphabetical-index.md) cover, and what a [candidate expression](candidate-expression.md) recurs across. A [golden corpus](golden-corpus.md) is a fixed corpus with the model the engine must produce from it.
