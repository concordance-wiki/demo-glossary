# Cue

One timed unit of a [transcript](transcript.md): a start and an end timecode and the words spoken between them. A cue is the position of a word read in a transcript, as a page or a slide is in a converted document: a [mention](mention.md) cites it by its timecode, and the rail of the document viewer lists the cues with the first line of their text.
