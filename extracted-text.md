# Extracted text

The words of a [document](document.md) as the build reads them: the text of every page of the PDF the converter produced, or of the PDF given as a source, and the cues of a transcript with their timecodes. An office document never gives its text any other way, so that one extraction path serves every format. The extracted text feeds the [search index](search-index.md), the recognition of [occurrences](occurrence.md) and the comparison of [twin resources](twin-resources.md), each word keeping the page, slide or cue it was read on, so that a [mention](mention.md) cites `slide 3` where a [note](note.md) cites a line. The page of the document shows it position by position, cut at the configured size; the download gives the rest.
