# Facet

A way of narrowing the results of the [search](search-index.md) by one property of the [entities](entity.md): their type, their [source](source.md), their domain, their application, or whether a [note](note.md) defines them. The results page lists every value the [site](site.md) knows with the number of results carrying it, counted in the browser over the current query; the selected values combine, a value nothing would come of stays listed at zero rather than vanishing, and the choice travels in the address of the page so that a search can be sent as a link.

## Not to be confused with

The filter of the mentions panel, which narrows the mentions of one page by their text and has no count.
