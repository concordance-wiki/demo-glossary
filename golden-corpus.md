# Golden corpus

A fictional corpus about a generic personal insurer, in English and in French, with the [model](model.md) and [findings](finding.md) the engine must produce from it. Every story is tested against it; a change in the expected result goes through a reviewed pull request. This glossary and its companion specification repository are the public counterpart: real content about the tool itself.
