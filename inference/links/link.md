# Link

A typed relation between two [entities](../model/entity.md), carrying a [confidence](confidence.md) and at least one [provenance](provenance.md). A link is produced by a written [markdown link](markdown-link.md), a frontmatter reference, a mention in a mapped section, an [occurrence](../recognition/occurrence.md) or a co-occurrence.

## Not to be confused with

A [markdown link](markdown-link.md) written by an author, which is one of the ways a link gets produced.
