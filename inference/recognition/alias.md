# Alias

Another name of an [entity](../model/entity.md), declared under `aliases` in the frontmatter of its [note](../../ingestion/documents/note.md). An alias enters the [dictionary](dictionary.md) with the title and is recognised the same way, compared without regard to case, accents or the plural; it weighs 4 in the search index where the title weighs 5. An alias that another entity carries as its title or alias makes the two [homonyms](homonym.md).
