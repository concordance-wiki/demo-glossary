# Comparison form

The shape in which two texts are compared: lower-cased, accents stripped, apostrophes unified, each word singularised by the suffix rules of the [language pack](language-pack.md). "Keyword Page", "keyword pages" and "keyword page" have one comparison form and are one [term](term.md). Titles and [aliases](alias.md) enter the [dictionary](dictionary.md) in this form, [stopwords](stopword.md) are compared in it, a [candidate expression](candidate-expression.md) is grouped by it, the search index stores every word with it, and the [twin resources](../../ingestion/sources/twin-resources.md) comparison reads texts in it. Hyphens and apostrophes stay part of a word.
