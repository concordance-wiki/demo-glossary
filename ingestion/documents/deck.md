# Deck

A slide presentation, a `.pptx` file that a [converter](converter.md) turns into a PDF at build so that the site previews it and reads its text slide by slide. Its page offers the original for download, the PDF the conversion produced or the [preview](preview.md) committed next to it, a rail of its slides captioned by their first line and the [extracted text](extracted-text.md) of every slide; a mention read in a deck cites its slide. A deck is often a [twin resource](../sources/twin-resources.md) of the notes and the transcript of the session where it was shown.
