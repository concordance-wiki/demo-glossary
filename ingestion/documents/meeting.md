# Meeting

A working session the documentation keeps as a note typed `meeting`: its minutes, with the date, the participants and the decision the session produced under `decisions`, the [transcript](transcript.md) of what was said and, when one was shown, the [deck](deck.md), grouped by the build as [twin resources](../sources/twin-resources.md) into one page with a tab per file. A meeting brings passages, not concepts: it enters the model through the pages it mentions and through the decision someone wrote as a note of its own, which the page names in a callout.
