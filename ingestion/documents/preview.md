# Preview

The PDF form of an office document, produced by the [conversion](conversion.md) at build and cached by fingerprint, or committed next to the original by its author; either way the page of the document opens it in the viewer on demand and the original stays downloadable. `previews: false` on a [source](../sources/source.md) keeps the PDFs out of the published site: the download link and the extracted text remain. A committed preview shares the base name of its original and repeats its text, two of the signals that make them [twin resources](../sources/twin-resources.md).
