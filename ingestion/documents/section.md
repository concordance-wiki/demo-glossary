# Section

A part of a [note](note.md) under a heading of level one or two: the [fragment](../../publication/site/fragment.md) of the note holds one rendered section per heading, every [occurrence](../../inference/recognition/occurrence.md) records the section it was read in, and a section whose heading the [profile](../configuration/profile.md) maps to a relation turns the notes named in it into [section mentions](../../inference/recognition/section-mention.md). Headings are matched without regard to case or accents, in the language of the source.
