# Corpus

The notes and documents of every [source](source.md) a [build](../pipeline/build.md) reads together: what the [dictionary](../../inference/recognition/dictionary.md), the [search index](../../publication/search/search-index.md) and the [alphabetical index](../../publication/search/alphabetical-index.md) cover, and what a [candidate expression](../../inference/recognition/candidate-expression.md) recurs across. A [golden corpus](../../quality/linter/golden-corpus.md) is a fixed corpus with the model the engine must produce from it.
