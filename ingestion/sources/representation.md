# Representation

One of the files an [entity](../../inference/model/entity.md) is made of: its [note](../documents/note.md), a slide deck, the PDF a [converter](../documents/converter.md) produced from it, a [transcript](../documents/transcript.md), the contract an operation was imported from. A note alone has one representation; [twin resources](twin-resources.md) merged into one entity carry several, and its page shows them one after the other, the file for download, its extracted text and its viewer. An office document without markdown next to it is reported by `W-DOC-NOMD` until a note becomes its representation.
