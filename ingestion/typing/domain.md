# Domain

A business grouping declared globally, by the name of a folder of the corpus (a [folder domain](folder-domain.md)) or by glob patterns evaluated across every [source](../sources/source.md). Repositories are usually split by kind of document; domains cut across them. A note outside every domain lands in the unclassified domain and gets a [finding](../../quality/findings/finding.md).
