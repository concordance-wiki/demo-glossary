# Rule

An [entity](../../inference/model/entity.md) of [type](type.md) `rule`: a constraint with a severity and a condition, and an `## Applies to` section naming the screens, processes, objects or APIs it constrains. The rules of this wiki describe the [checks](../../quality/checks/check.md) of the tool, one note per check, and the rule that binds the linter to the build.

## Not to be confused with

A typing rule of a [source](../sources/source.md), the `match` and `set` pair of the configuration that gives a type to the files of a folder.
