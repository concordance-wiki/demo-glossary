# Keyword page

The page of a word nobody defined. It uses the same template as a [note](note.md), without the markdown and the declared properties: a banner says no note exists, three numbers give the occurrences, files and sources, and the passages follow, grouped by file. A keyword page exists from the [publication threshold](publication-threshold.md), three [occurrences](occurrence.md) in at least two files. If someone writes the note later, the page keeps its address and fills in.
