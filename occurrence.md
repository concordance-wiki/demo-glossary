# Occurrence

One place where a word of the dictionary appears in a file: the file, the line, the position, the enclosing section and eighty characters of context. Occurrences are what the tool records first; everything else, [links](link.md), [keyword pages](keyword-page.md), the to-do page, derives from them. Code blocks, URLs, frontmatter and link targets never yield occurrences.
