# Passage

The words around an [occurrence](occurrence.md) as a page quotes them, eighty characters kept at the scan with the matched words marked: a [keyword page](keyword-page.md) lists its passages grouped by file in corpus order, each linked to its line, and the mentions panel shows one under every recognised [mention](mention.md). The passages of a keyword page live in its [fragment](fragment.md).
