# Alphabetical index

The page of the [site](../site/site.md), under `index/`, that lists every word with or without a note by initial letter, in the collation of the [language pack](../../inference/recognition/language-pack.md) of the project locale: a bar of letters first, a letter without an entry inactive, each entry with the glyph of its [type](../../ingestion/typing/type.md) or the "no note" mark and its citation count. When the whole index would weigh more than 100 kB as one page, every letter gets its own page. The home page shows the letters with their counts as one of its three entry points.

## Not to be confused with

The [search index](search-index.md), the files under `search/` that the search field loads and no reader opens.
