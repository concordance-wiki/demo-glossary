# Passage

The words around an [occurrence](../../inference/recognition/occurrence.md) as a page quotes them, eighty characters kept at the scan with the matched words marked: a [keyword page](../../inference/recognition/keyword-page.md) lists its passages grouped by file in corpus order, each linked to its line, and the related pages of an entity show one under every citing page, the [mention](../../inference/recognition/mention.md) an author wrote when there is one. The passages of a keyword page live in its [fragment](fragment.md).
