# Pinned page

A page of the [site](site.md) a reader chose to keep at hand: pinned from the button after its title, shown as a tab in a row under the bar of every page, in the order of pinning, until the reader removes it. The row exists only once a page is pinned; it holds what fits and folds the rest behind a summary that lists every pin with a filter. The pins live in the browser, never in the address, and never expire on their own: an [entity](../../inference/model/entity.md) pinned keeps its tab, named by the title read at the time, even once a later publication removed its page. Without JavaScript the row and the button are not there: the served HTML carries every page in full, as the [theme](theme.md) renders it.
