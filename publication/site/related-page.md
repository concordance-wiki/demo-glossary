# Related page

A page that evokes an [entity](../../inference/model/entity.md), as the right panel of that entity's page lists it: its title, its type, the number of [passages](passage.md) where the entity is named and one of those passages as an excerpt. The related pages of an entity are ordered by number of passages, a [link](../../inference/links/markdown-link.md) an author wrote and a [recognised word](../../inference/recognition/recognised-word.md) counted alike; a page that writes a link is marked "cited", because a link is what someone asserted and a recognised word what the tool inferred. The block groups the [mentions](../../inference/recognition/mention.md) of the entity by citing page and can be narrowed by type or by a word once the page's script runs.
