# Check family

The grouping of the [checks](check.md) in their documentation: every check page states its family, and the list of the checks is laid out by family, [source checks](source-checks.md), [link checks](link-checks.md), [identifier and type checks](identifier-and-type-checks.md), [document checks](document-checks.md), [vocabulary and filing checks](vocabulary-and-filing-checks.md), [contract checks](contract-checks.md) and [plugin checks](plugin-checks.md). The family says which step of the pipeline raises the [finding](../findings/finding.md) and which note or file it points at.
