# Recognised word

A word of the text of a [note](note.md) that the occurrence scan matched against the dictionary: a title or an alias of an [entity](entity.md), read in a paragraph, a list item, a table cell or a quote. On the entity page the build turns it into a link to the page of the entity it names, drawn with a dotted underline so that it is told apart from a [link](markdown-link.md) the author wrote, and a legend under the text says which is which. A recognised word carries less weight than a written link: it is what the tool found, not what someone asserted, which is why the two are never drawn alike.

## Not to be confused with

An [occurrence](occurrence.md), which is the record of the match in the model; the recognised word is its mark in the rendered page.
