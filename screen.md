# Screen

An [entity](entity.md) of [type](type.md) `screen`: a page of an application, with the roles that use it, the objects it reads and writes, its actions leading to other screens and the rules that constrain it, declared in its [frontmatter](frontmatter.md) or under its `## Objects`, `## Actions` and `## Rules` sections. The screens of this wiki are the pages of the generated [site](site.md), one note per page slot, each with a `## Today` section that says what the page renders in this version.
