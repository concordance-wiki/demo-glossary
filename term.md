# Term

A [note](note.md) of a glossary source: the H1 is the term, the first paragraph its definition, `aliases` its other names and `broader` the term it specialises. Every title and [alias](alias.md) of a term enters the [dictionary](dictionary.md) first, so that its [occurrences](occurrence.md) in every other note link to it; a term never cited yields `W-TERM-UNUSED`, and a form it shares with another entity makes them [homonyms](homonym.md). A term is short and exact; a `## Not to be confused with` section settles a homonym.
