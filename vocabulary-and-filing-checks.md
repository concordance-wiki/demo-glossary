# Vocabulary and filing checks

The [check family](check-family.md) of the words and of the containers: `W-TERM-UNDEFINED`, a [candidate expression](candidate-expression.md) used across files without a note; `W-TERM-UNUSED`, a [term](term.md) never cited; `I-TERM-HOMONYM`, two entities sharing a form; `W-DOMAIN-UNCLASSIFIED` and `W-DOMAIN-UNKNOWN`, a note outside every [domain](domain.md) or naming an undeclared one; `W-APP-MISSING` and `W-APP-UNKNOWN`, the same for the [application](application.md); `W-STALE`, a source or a note past the [staleness](staleness.md) threshold; `I-REL-AMBIGUOUS`, a link left on the generic relation; `I-PII-DETECTED`, a personal name found in a transcript outside the [pseudonymisation](pseudonymisation.md) dictionary.
