# White label

A site generated for an organisation that shows the organisation's name, logo, colours and fonts and nothing of the tool that built it. Everything a reader sees comes from the [theme](theme.md); the credit of the tool in the footer is off unless the project turns it on. The project's own wiki turns it on, since the tool is documenting itself.
